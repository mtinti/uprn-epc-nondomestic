# Data dictionary: `epc_nondomestic`, schema `epc_nondomestic_v1`

One row per published non-domestic Energy Performance Certificate in Scotland, superseded certificates
included. Key: `epc_release`, `source_row`. Three groups of columns in this order: the key and provenance
added by the build, the 38 published columns as text exactly as published under the publisher's names,
and the derived columns, each a typed copy of one published column by one rule.
Generated by `python -m epc_nd_ingest.dictionary`; do not edit by hand.

Current build: release 2026Q3, 95,719 rows; 57 columns in the Parquet, 57 in the CSV; built 2026-11-14T18:46:18Z.

## Conventions

- Publisher: Scottish Government, EPCenquiries@gov.scot. Licence: Open Government Licence v3.0 for every field except ADDRESS1, ADDRESS2, POST_TOWN and POSTCODE, which carry a Royal Mail copyright notice: use for energy-efficiency purposes permitted by the notice, and the notice must accompany any sharing (publication note, page 2).
- `HISTORIC = True` means the certificate has been superseded by a later one for the same property, not that it has expired. A certificate is valid for ten years.
- The rating and the band rate emissions, lower is better. They are not the domestic rating and band. Compare the two registers only on the floor area, the emission rate and the primary energy value.
- The published columns are never NULL: an empty cell is the empty string, as published. The derived columns are NULL only where the dictionary below says so.
- Join by `uprn_osg` as an integer, never as text: a cohort UPRN padded to 12 digits matches once both sides are bigint. The published `OSG_REFERENCE_NUMBER` text is provenance only.
- Most non-domestic buildings have never had a certificate: one is required only on construction, sale or let, and for display in a public building over 250 square metres. A cohort row with no match is `uprn_not_in_epc`, not an error. A third of the certificates have no UPRN.
- Every column of the Parquet is in the CSV and the SQL table, the three address lines included (`epc_nd_ingest/export_contract.yaml`, decision `address-lines-loaded`). Nothing built leaves the safe haven.
- A text column accepts values up to its width. SQL Server stores it as `nvarchar(width)`, or as `nvarchar(max)` when the width is over 4,000 (decision `width-is-accepted-length`). Widths are counted as SQL Server counts them, in UTF-16 code units: an emoji or another character outside the Basic Multilingual Plane counts as two.
- Column names are case-sensitive in the Parquet; in SQL Server they are compared under the database collation, which is why no derived name equals a published name in another case.

## Values that are codes, not measurements

Found in the profile of release 2026Q3 and kept as published.

- `Data removed` in `POTENTIAL_ENERGY_PERFORMANCE_RATING` and its band (1,712 rows) and in `ASSET_RATING` and its band (12,484 rows). It is the publisher's missing value. The note ties it to lodgements before 20 March 2016, but `ASSET_RATING` carries it in every year to 2026.
- `Carbon Neu` in the two band columns: the file cuts the band text at ten characters. It means carbon neutral, a rating below zero. Four rows carry it with a rating of 0.
- The rating and the band rate emissions (kg of CO2 per square metre per year), lower is better, on a scale A+ to G with plus grades. They are not the domestic rating and band, which score energy cost and where higher is better. The same letter means different things in the two registers.
- `-0` in `NEW_BUILD_ENERGY_PERFORMANCE_RATING` in 25 rows.
- Negative values in the rating, emission and primary energy columns, for buildings that generate more than they use. `CURRENT_ENERGY_PERFORMANCE_RATING` runs from -9,098 to 42,155.
- `FLOOR_AREA` runs from 4 to 138,037 square metres. It is the conditioned area of the building, not the area of a dwelling.
- `CURRENT_ENERGY_PERFORMANCE_RATING` is `BUILDING_EMISSIONS` rounded, except in 473 rows where it differs by one.
- `MEETS_2002_STANDARD` is `Y`, `N` or empty. It is empty for lodgements before 20 March 2016, with two exceptions.
- `ELECTRICITY_SOURCE` is `Grid supplied` in every row.
- `PROPERTY_TYPE` mixes two vocabularies, with and without a use-class prefix (43 values). `PROPERTY_TYPE_SHORT` folds them into 23, among them both `Miscellaneous 24 hr activity` and `Misc. 24 hr activity`. Neither column separates a hospital from a care home.
- `LOCAL_AUTHORITY` holds old-style codes such as `00QS`. Ten rows carry a code of an English authority. `LOCAL_AUTHORITY`, `DATA_ZONE` and `CONSTITUENCY` are empty together in 2,553 rows.
- `DATA_ZONE` holds 294 values such as `00QQMB ()` that are not data zones, and `DATA_ZONE_2011` holds `NA (NA)` in 40 rows.
- `CONSTITUENCY` and `CONSTITUENCY_LABEL` hold the ward code and name, as the publisher's labels say.
- 517 address cells and 5 postcodes contain a tab. One `ADDRESS1` cell holds the replacement character U+FFFD. 93,392 `IMPROVEMENT_RECOMMENDATIONS` cells end with a space. All are kept as published.
- One publisher's label carries the replacement character U+FFFD (�) where the publisher's export lost a `²`. The label is kept as the file prints it.
- `IMPROVEMENT_RECOMMENDATIONS` is a `|`-separated list of measures, each as code, CO2 impact, payback type and description. It reaches 4,451 characters.
